Customer Demographics & Revenue Analytics

Tableau | SQL | Advanced Excel

Tableau SQL Excel

Project Overview & Objectives

Understanding the underlying demographics driving major revenue streams is critical for targeted marketing and customer retention. This project involved analyzing a massive transactional dataset to uncover purchasing behaviors across different customer segments.

Objective: Process and visualize $986M+ in revenue data across 150,000+ orders to identify key demographic drivers such as age, occupation, and cuisine preferences, ultimately building an interactive Tableau dashboard for executive stakeholders.

Data Processing & Visualization Strategy

The raw data was initially queried using SQL to aggregate sales by customer ID and demographic categories. Excel was used for preliminary data cleaning and exploratory data analysis (EDA) before connecting the dataset to Tableau. The dashboard was engineered to feature dynamic filtering, allowing users to drill down into specific months, age brackets, or occupation types.

Key Findings & Business Insights

  • High-Value Segments: Identified the specific occupation groups that contribute the highest Average Order Value (AOV).
  • Trend Identification: Mapped out monthly revenue trends, revealing seasonal spikes and dips in purchasing behaviors.
  • Cuisine Preferences: Correlated customer age demographics with specific cuisine preferences to help tailor future product offerings and promotional campaigns.
